Use this form to request access to the [Livestream API](/livestream-api/introduction). Submissions are reviewed by the X Developer Platform team. Use a company email and provide accurate application details so we can evaluate eligibility and follow up. Incomplete submissions may delay review.

  The form opens in a new tab and requires a Google account sign-in.
</Card>

## What the form asks for

Have the following information ready before you start:

- Your name, company email, and job title
- Organization name and website URL
- The numeric **X Developer App ID** of the app that will call the Livestream API, from the [Developer Console](https://console.x.com)
- Your current X API plan
- A description of your product and how you will use the Livestream API
- Approximate monthly active users or customers

<Note>
  Livestream API access is subject to review and may require an Enterprise or custom commercial agreement. By submitting the form you agree to comply with the [X Developer Agreement and Policy](/developer-terms) and applicable API terms. If you already have an Enterprise agreement, ask your X account team to enable the Livestream API for your app.
</Note>
