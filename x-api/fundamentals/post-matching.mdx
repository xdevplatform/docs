This page explains which content filtered stream rules match on for every kind of Post.

---

## The rule

**Keyword, phrase, and entity operators match the content a Post carries.** That includes the full text of long posts, not only the first 280 characters.

**Quote Posts and Reposts carry the Post they share.** A Quote Post matches on its own content and the quoted Post's content. A Repost matches on the original Post.

**Replies match on their own content only.** A reply does not include the Post it replies to. To match the replied-to Post, use `in_reply_to_text:`.

Limiting replies to their own content removes matches that came only from the replied-to Post. It also adds matches for some negated rules:

- `keyword -is:reply` starts matching Quote Posts of replies. A Quote Post of a reply is not itself a reply.
- `keyword -has:links` (and other exclusions such as `-has:images` or `-is:nullcast`) starts matching a reply when the excluded property is on the replied-to Post and not on the reply.

---

## What each Post type matches on

| Post type | Keywords, phrases, `#`, `$`, `@`, `url:` match | Use to match the referenced Post |
|:----------|:-----------------------------------------------|:---------------------------------|
| Original Post | The Post's text and entities | — |
| Long post (over 280 characters) | The full text in `note_tweet.text` and the entities across the full text | — |
| Reply | The reply's own text and entities, including the @handles it replies to | `in_reply_to_text:` |
| Quote Post | The Quote Post's own text and entities, and the quoted Post's text and entities | `quoted_text:` (quoted Post only) |
| Repost | The original Post's full text and entities | — |
| Article | The Article's title and body | — |

Reply metadata operators such as `to:`, `in_reply_to_tweet_id:`, `is:reply`, and `is:quote` are unaffected.

## Matching beyond 280 characters

Long posts deliver the first part of their text in `text` and the full text in `note_tweet.text`. Matching uses the full text, so a rule can match a word that does not appear in `text`.

To see the full text in the stream, request `tweet.fields=note_tweet`.

```bash
curl "https://api.x.com/2/tweets/search/stream?tweet.fields=note_tweet" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $BEARER_TOKEN"
```

## Matching the referenced Post

Use these operators to match content in the Post being quoted or replied to:

| Operator | Matches | Example |
|:---------|:--------|:--------|
| `quoted_text:` | The text of the directly quoted Post | `quoted_text:launch` |
| `in_reply_to_text:` | The text of the directly replied-to Post | `in_reply_to_text:"product update"` |

Both operators:
- Take a keyword or a quoted phrase, tokenized the same way as a bare keyword.
- Match the full text when the referenced Post is a long post.
- Are standalone and can be negated, for example `-quoted_text:spoiler`.

Examples:

| Goal | Rule |
|:-----|:-----|
| A keyword in a reply or in the Post it replies to | `acme OR in_reply_to_text:acme` |
| A keyword only in the quoted Post, not the Quote Post's own text | `quoted_text:acme` |
| Quote Posts about a topic, excluding quotes of Posts with a word | `launch is:quote -quoted_text:giveaway` |

To receive the referenced Post in the stream, use `expansions=referenced_tweets.id`.

## Search Posts

[Search Posts](/x-api/posts/search/introduction) matches replies on their own text and long posts on their full text. It differs from filtered stream in these ways:

| Behavior | Filtered stream | Search Posts |
|:---------|:----------------|:-------------|
| Quote Posts | Match on the Quote Post and the quoted Post | Match on the Quote Post's own text |
| Reposts | Match on the original Post's full text | Match on the Repost's own text |
| `quoted_text:`, `in_reply_to_text:` | Supported | Not supported |
| Diacritics | Keywords with accents match only Posts with the accent | Keywords with accents match Posts with and without the accent |

See [Filtered stream operators](/x-api/posts/filtered-stream/integrate/operators) for the full operator list.
