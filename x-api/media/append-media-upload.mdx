---
openapi: post /2/media/upload/{id}/append
---

Upload one segment of a chunked file. Call this after [`POST /2/media/upload/initialize`](/x-api/media/initialize-media-upload). Keep each segment at or below **5 MB** (server maximum is 8 MB). `segment_index` starts at `0`.

See the [chunked upload quickstart](/x-api/media/quickstart/media-upload-chunked).
