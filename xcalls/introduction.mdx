<Warning>
  The X Calls API is in development. It isn't generally available yet, and endpoints and payloads may still change.
</Warning>

The X Calls API puts a voice agent in an X call. Your agent joins as your app's user, typically a bot account, hears everyone in the call and talks back, like a person in the X app. Use it to:

- **Call people**: start a call in a conversation with your bot; X rings the other members and your agent greets whoever picks up.
- **Host calls**: start a call, share its link, and let your agent run it, admitting people if the call is closed.
- **Join calls** your bot was given the link to: a meeting assistant, an interpreter, a game host.
- **Schedule calls** on your user's X Calls calendar, and find the calls on it to join.

You bring the agent: a speech-to-speech model, or speech-to-text → LLM → text-to-speech, connected to a WebSocket that streams raw audio both ways. X handles everything about the call itself.

Endpoints are hosted under `https://api.x.com`.

## How it works

```text
 your app                        X API                            X call
 ────────                        ─────                            ──────
 POST /2/calls ───────────────▶  create the call ───────────────▶ (empty call)
 POST /2/calls/:id/join ──────▶  admit your user, mint a
          ◀── media_url + media_token (single use, ~2 min)
 WebSocket media_url ─────────▶  your user enters the call ─────▶ participants see
   (Bearer media_token)                                           and hear your bot
   ◀── binary: call audio (PCM16) ──────────────────────────────── everyone else
   ─── binary: agent audio (PCM16) ───────────────────────────────▶ everyone hears it
   ◀── text: events (participant.joined, speech.started, …)
 POST /2/calls/:id/leave ─────▶  (or send {"type":"leave"}, or close the socket)
```

## Core concepts

- **Call**: has a host, a join policy and a short id such as `t69-8sb-6z5`. Its link is `https://call.x.com/{id}`. Anyone with the id can open it, so treat the id like a secret.
- **Media session**: joining starts a media session for your user. Your user enters the call when your WebSocket connects and leaves when it closes. A user has one session per call.
- **Audio**: plain PCM on the WebSocket, 16-bit little-endian mono at the sample rate you choose (8, 16, 24 or 48 kHz). X handles WebRTC, Opus, jitter and mixing: you receive one mixed stream of everyone else (never your own agent) and send one stream back.
- **Scheduled call**: an X call on the X Calls calendar, with a time, a title, the people invited, and optionally a recurrence. Scheduling creates the call right away, so its link can be shared at once.

### Join policies

The join policy decides who gets in:

| Policy | Who joins directly |
|:-------|:-------------------|
| `open` | Anyone with the id, including people who are not logged in. |
| `closed` | The host, people the host admitted, and people already in the call. Everyone else asks to join. |
| `trusted` | Like `closed`, plus members of the host's Verified Organization. |

When your bot must be admitted, the join response says `"admission": "requires_approval"` and your WebSocket receives `session.waiting` until the host answers.

### Key identifiers

| Identifier | Example | What it is |
|:-----------|:--------|:-----------|
| Call id | `t69-8sb-6z5` | Identifies a call (`id`, or `call_id` on a scheduled call). Also its join secret. |
| Session id | `cs_4f1c2a9e0b7d3a51` | Identifies a media session in events and logs. |
| Media token | `eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9…` | Single-use credential for the media WebSocket, valid about 2 minutes. |
| Scheduled call id | `1840000000000000000` | Identifies a call on the X Calls calendar: what change, cancel and rsvp take. It is not the call id. |
| Conversation id | `<user_id>-<user_id>`, `g…` | The XChat conversation a call is started in: a 1:1 or a group conversation. |
