<Warning>
  The X Calls API is in development. It isn't generally available yet, and endpoints and payloads may still change.
</Warning>

## Calling someone

Start the call in your bot's conversation with them, then join it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the call in the conversation">
    `POST /2/calls` with `conversation_id`: a 1:1 id (`<user_id>-<user_id>`, as the chat endpoints return it, or `<user_id>:<user_id>`) or a group id (`g…`). Your bot must be a member of the conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join it and connect your agent">
    `POST /2/calls/:id/join`, then open the [media WebSocket](/xcalls/media-websocket).
  </Step>
  <Step title="X rings the other members">
    When the host (your bot) enters the empty call, X rings the other members: in a group, every other member; in a 1:1 conversation, the other person, if X's messaging trust check allows your account to reach them. Ringing is a VoIP push, which reaches the X and XChat apps on iOS; people on other devices join from the conversation or the call link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If nobody joins within 60 seconds, your agent's session ends with `alone`.

If the conversation already has a call in progress, `POST /2/calls` returns **that** call instead of a new one: its `host_id` is whoever started it, and its title and policy are theirs.

## Answering a call

X does not notify bots of incoming calls yet. When you know someone has started a call in a conversation with your bot, for example because they messaged your bot, `POST /2/calls` with that conversation's id returns the call in progress (`host_id` is the caller), and joining it answers.

Conversation ids are matched exactly, so use the id in the form the caller's call uses (the chat endpoints return 1:1 ids as `<user_id>-<user_id>`). If no call is in progress, the request starts a new one.

## Hosting a closed call

When your bot hosts a `closed` or `trusted` call, people who are not admitted automatically ask to join. Your agent receives `join.requested` and answers with `join.admit` or `join.reject`:

```json
{ "type": "join.admit", "user_id": "783214" }
```

Requests made while your agent is not in the call are delivered once it joins.
